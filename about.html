About Gaoteng Energy

30 years of power electronics experience — from cells to complete systems.

Who We Are

Engineering Reliable Energy for the World

Gaoteng Energy is a manufacturer of hybrid inverters, monocrystalline solar panels and LiFePO4 battery storage. From a single cell to a megawatt-scale project, we design systems that keep homes and businesses powered.

Our vertically integrated factory in China produces, tests and delivers to more than 20 countries across Africa, Asia and the Middle East.

  • Vertically integrated manufacturing
  • In-house R&D and testing lab
  • Full export documentation & certification support
